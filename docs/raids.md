# Farm Raids (owner, 9 Nov 2026: "make the harvest ville like clash clan" ... "I like A, it's more intense game")

A Clash of Clans-like raid on another farm: you train raiders at your Raid Camp, drop them at the edge of a real farm,
and for 2 minutes they grab what is out in the open while that farm's defenses fight back. Stars 1-3.

## Decisions (owner, 9 Nov 2026)
- **Who:** everyone (friends too, for now: "We dont have lot of farm"). Pick a farm from a list (its power shown, a
  stronger one if you dare) or Random (the server picks one near your power). See the loot before you attack; Next
  finds another (a little Gold).
- **Farm Power** = raiders' levels + defenses' levels + farm level (weights adjustable).
- **Loot cap by power** (adjustable): the defender 1.5x your power or more: up to 30%; about the same: 20%; 2/3 of
  yours or less: 10%; sliding between. The stars decide how much of the cap you get.
- **Loot** = only what is out in the open: ripe crops in the field, uncollected eggs and milk, the market stall's
  shelves, workshop goods waiting, and a "today's earnings" Gold pot. Never the bag, chests, storage, Selebox Coins or
  Stars. Every raid also wins Raid Points for its stars (a ranking, weekly prizes, a little Gold from the game).
- **Every number lives on the server** and can be changed any time (caps, power weights, shields, limits, costs,
  strengths).
- **Protection:** an 8-hour shield after being raided (raiding drops it); new farms safe for 7 days; 5 raids a day,
  energy per raid; Revenge free.
- **Fair play:** the server picks the farm and sends a frozen copy + a seed; the phone plays it and sends its moves;
  the game server replays them and only then gives the loot and records the defender's loss (applied when they next
  open the game, with a report, Replay and Revenge). A farm is locked while it is raided.
