# Villager friendship stories (6 Nov 2026)

Every one of the 23 villagers in Willowbrook now has a short story in four parts. A part opens at **2, 4, 6 and 8
hearts**. The next time you talk to them (or meet them at the right place in their day), a small scene plays: a few
lines, then you pick what to say. What you say changes their answer, and sometimes the gift. Some parts ask for a
small favour (bring something) or a hand at their shop (it costs some energy). Every finished part gives a reward from
what the game already has. The last part (8 hearts) always tells you their secret.

The stories tie the town together: Leo and you fix Ewan's old boat before Ewan can sail it again, Elliot's surprise
waits until Clara finishes her book, Father Tomas learns who saved him in the storm only after Ewan tells you about
it, and many scenes have a second villager walking in (Finn, Rosa, Pearl, Bram, Daisy, Owen and others).

## How a player finds the next step

- **Pause > Friends** now opens the **Friendship Book**: every villager with their hearts, how far their story is
  ("Story 1 of 4"), and what to do next, for example "Meet Poppy at the gazebo (5 PM-7 PM)" or
  "Bring Rosa 6 Egg (you have 2)". A villager with a part ready right now has a red **NEW** tag and a green
  **Story** button.
- Each villager's page shows the four parts: finished ones with what you got (and **Read** to see the scene again,
  with the secret), the next one with what to do, and locked ones with a hint only, never the scene.
- **Photos** (top left of the Book) opens the Photo wall.
- When you talk to someone whose next part waits for a place, a time or energy, their normal line ends with a short
  note saying where and when ("Friendship Book: Mira wants to see you at the river dock (6 PM-7 PM)").
- Every villager's talk panel has a small **Story** button by their name.
- A part can start from talking in town, from a shop's **Talk** button (for parts "in" their shop), at the mayor's
  house, or by knocking at a home.
- A first-time tour explains the Book (the first time it opens, or after the first story part you finish).

## Rewards, in short

- **Recipes** (6, all things you could only buy before): Sandwich (Rosa), Energy Tonic (Ivy), Cookies (Pearl),
  Hot Soup (Father Tomas), Fresh Bread and Strawberry Cake (Hana). They appear at the end of the Kitchen's list.
- **Friend prices** (10% off, shown on the Buy buttons): Lily's seeds, Poppy's bouquets, Owen's animal clinic.
- **Photos** (9): each is a frame on the Photo wall and also a Painting in your house storage (the stand-in for the
  photo until it has its own picture).
- **Decor**: furniture goes to house storage, yard pieces to yard storage (Decorate to place them).
- **Costume pieces**: none. The Tailor Shop only has whole looks, sold through the server, so no story gives one.
