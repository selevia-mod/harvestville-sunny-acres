# Town Raid v2: every night, and rewards for the defenders themselves (owner, 9 Nov 2026)

## When
Every day, 8:00 to 8:30 PM Manila (was Monday and Thursday). The countdown chip shows from 7:30.

## What every player gets
- **Every night they help:** the help chest (as now), **Defender rank** points, **Night streak** +1.
- **Defender rank** (all-time, never resets): Farmhand → Watcher → Guard → Captain → Hero of Harvest Ville. Shown
  under the player's name wherever others see them (visits, hunting places, chat).
- **Night streak:** "🔥 12 nights in a row" on the farm card; a missed night starts it over.
- **Hero of the Night:** the night's top 3 defenders, on the town board, named in the next day's town message.
- **The town wins the night:** a festival banner in the town square until the next raid, and "You helped save Harvest
  Ville tonight." The Double Drops bonus day is gone.

The prizes are given by the server only (a claim, once, by the month's final board); Selebox Stars go to the app
wallet the same way the game's other server-side credits do.
