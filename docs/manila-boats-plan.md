# Manila Bay boats: a plan for the owner to think over (18 Nov 2026)

Owner, 18 Nov 2026: "9, lets plan it (make it last so I will think of it first)". Nothing here is built. Today the pier
has 3 empty berths with signs ("Boats are coming to Manila Bay soon!"), the Harbor building says "coming soon", and
Kapitana Marisol (harbor master, a Manila Friend: at 6 hearts she opens a better fishing spot on the pier) is waiting.

## What boats could be (pick any; my suggestion first)
1. **Deep-sea fishing trips (suggested first).** Board Kapitana Marisol's fishing boat at berth 1: a short trip out to
   sea (its own small map: open water, the boat's deck, a few fishing spots that move). New sea fish that only live
   out there (tuna, lapu-lapu, maya-maya, a rare marlin as the trip's "boss fish"), for the Kitchen, the Fishing Derby
   and the Hunter's/Fishing book. A trip lasts a set time (e.g. 10 real minutes) and costs a ticket (gold).
2. **The sunset cruise.** A pleasure ride round the bay at 5-7 PM: the city skyline, the lighthouse, the sunset glow;
   photos for the Photo wall; a friend can ride with you (online). Cheap ticket, no rewards beyond the photos and a
   little friendship with whoever rides with you.
3. **Island trips.** A small island across the bay (its own map): a beach, coconuts, shells to forage, a hidden chest
   a day, a lighthouse keeper with a short story. A ferry from berth 2.
4. **Your own boat.** Like the trucks: a small banca made at Bram's Forge, then bigger boats with Market Stars; your
   boat sits at berth 3 when you are in Manila; it is what takes you on 1-3 without a ticket.
5. **Bay events.** A weekly boat race (like the Manila Cup, rowing), or a fishing contest at sea on weekends.

## My suggested order
Step 1: the deep-sea trip (1) with tickets. Step 2: the sunset cruise (2). Step 3: your own boat (4) replacing tickets.
Islands (3) and events (5) later, once 1-2 are liked.

## Questions for you
1. Which of the 5 do you want, and in what order?
2. Tickets (pay each trip) or your own boat from the start?
3. New sea fish: how many, and should any be a "boss fish" with a medal?
4. Can friends ride together on a trip (needs the game server's rooms, like the hunting places)?
5. Does the horse/truck rule apply (no energy cost to ride; only fishing costs what fishing costs today)?

Kapitana Marisol's fishing boat and the cruise boat (side and top views, about the size of the jeepney), the deck as
a map piece, sea water and waves, 4-6 sea fish icons and sprites, an island beach set (if 3), a banca and bigger boats
(if 4). Sea creatures drawn cute, like the hunting grounds.
