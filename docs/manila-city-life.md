# Manila city life: make it a real city (owner, 11 Nov 2026)

Owner: "I want it to be like a real city Manila, we dont need to name it but we can replicate what we have there."
No real names, brands, logos or seals; real Manila is only the inspiration.

## Owner's decisions (11 Nov 2026)
1. The jeepney: players ride it and roam the city ("I like it, they can ride the jeep and roam around the city").
2. The Mall: players go inside, with people there: roamers, a security guard and a sales lady.
3. The Ferris wheel and Chinatown: yes ("Lets add that also").

## The plan (proposed by me, accepted by the owner)
### A city, not a park
- Most of the grass becomes city ground: asphalt road, sidewalks, concrete. Rows of plain 2-storey shophouses (store below, home above) fill the blocks between the real buildings; they don't open.
- One jeepney loop road round the whole city, sidewalks on both sides, crosswalks at the corners. People walk on sidewalks, the jeep on the road.

### The jeepney
- One colourful jeep (chrome, a horse on the hood, a route board) drives the loop all day.
- 6 waiting sheds (bench and roof): Station, Plaza, Talipapa, College, Mall, Bay Walk. It stops a few seconds at each; 1-3 city folk get on or off. No timetable: round and round.
- Players: tap "Para po" at a shed, pay 15G, ride, and get off at the stop they pick. Later it also runs to the three hunting-ground gates.
- If a player stands in front of it, it stops and honks ("beep beep"); it never runs anyone over. At night every other loop, lights on.
