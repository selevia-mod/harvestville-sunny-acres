# Hunter's Path: cards, gear sets, places 1–5

Draft for the owner, 6 Nov 2026. Approved already: Taming, the Hunter's Album, the World Boss, Hunter Rank, the daily
boss chest, exploring. This file is the two lists the owner asked for (cards, gear sets) and the first five places.

**The rules we keep**
- Coins never buy power: cards, gear and tames only come from playing.
- Nothing you miss is gone for good.
- Every grind ends in something you can see.

---

## How gear and cards work

- **Your hunter has 5 slots:** Bow · Head · Body · Feet · Charm.
- **Each piece of gear has 1 card hole.** A full set's Charm has 2.
- **Cards fit one kind of slot.** A Bow card only goes in the Bow, and so on.
- **Putting a card in is free. Taking it out costs Gold**, and the card comes back to you.
- **The same card twice does not stack.** Different cards do.
- **Today's gear keeps working and gets a card hole:**
  - Camo Cloak → Body
  - Quiet Boots → Feet
  - Fire Charm → Charm
  - The Big Quiver stays as it is, with no slot.
- **Gear is made by Kanor** at his Lodge, from what each place gives. Each place has its own set of 4: Head, Body, Feet, Charm.
- **Set bonus:** wear 2 pieces of a set for the small bonus, and all 4 for the big one.

### How often cards drop

| Who | Card chance |
|---|---|
| A regular animal | 1 in 200 |
| A rare animal (White Stag, Golden Boar, …) | 1 in 25 |
| A dungeon creature | 1 in 150 |
| A floor guard (floor 5, and the Lair's every 10th) | 1 in 40 |
| A boss (floor 10, the Bakunawa) | 1 in 15 |

A card is a picture of its creature. It goes in the Hunter's Album and can be sold at the Market. When you find one, friends hear about it.

### What can be tamed (Taming)

Feed one its favourite food a few times while it is calm (not charging) and it follows you like your dog. You have one
companion at a time, and the rest wait at the Lodge.

| Companion | From | Favourite food | Feedings | Its skill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rabbit | Pulag | Carrot | 5 | Finds 1 extra herb on the way |
| Deer | Pulag | Apple | 8 | Shy animals notice you later |
| Wild Duck | Banaue | Rice | 6 | Spots flying animals early |
| Wild Goat | Chocolate Hills | Cabbage | 8 | Carries 5 more things for you |
| Unggoy | Sierra Madre | Banana | 10 | Picks up drops for you |
| Paniki | Palawan | Mango | 10 | Finds hidden chests |
| **Golden Duck, Cocoa Goat, White Tagak, Silver Carabao (rare)** | their places | their place's best crop | 15 | The skill above, twice as strong, and it glows |

Animals that charge (boars, bears, crocodiles) and the dungeon creatures can't be tamed. Bosses never can.

### The World Boss (approved)

- **When:** twice a week at set times (like Double Drops), on one of the hunting maps, for everyone online.
- **Rewards:** everyone who lands 3 hits gets a chest, and the top 3 by hits get a special card or frame.
- **Who:** a different boss each week, from the places that are open.

### Hunter Rank (approved): not the same as Beauty

- **Separate from Beauty.** Beauty ranks how nice your house is. Hunter Rank ranks your hunting.
- **Points:** hunts, bosses, cards, the Album, and World Boss hits.
- **Ranks:** Bronze, Silver, Gold, Champion, then a monthly top 100.
- **Rewards:** a name frame and a title. Only looks, never power.
- **Monthly reset:** it starts over each month, and your best rank stays on your card.
