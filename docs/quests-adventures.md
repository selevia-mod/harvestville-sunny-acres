## The day (e45)
- 6 daily quests: 3 normal (today's easy kind, each with its villager's reason and Go) + 3 adventures.
- Any 5 claimed = the day's chest (today's "all 10": 1 Star + 1,000G). All 6 = 1 more Star.
- A day whose chest opened = a **good day**.

## Adventures
- A villager asks for a **quest item**: it exists nowhere else, shows only while that quest is on, can't be sold,
  gifted, shelved or traded, and is gone at the day's end (with the quest) if not delivered.
- Go: the dog leads (via Kanor's jeep to a place), you get the item there, then the dog leads you back to the villager:
  hand it over, a thank-you line, the prize + hearts with them.
- **Find**: sparkling spots in that place (1 to 3 pieces); the dog sniffs them out.
- **Hunt**: one creature there wears a small quest mark and drops it when caught.
- Prizes: find 1 piece = the medium prize (gold or a Treat); find 3 or hunt = a hard prize (Power-up, Star or Pet Meal).
- Only places you have opened. A farm with none gets the 3 starter adventures (farm, town, mine).
