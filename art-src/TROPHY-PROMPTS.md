# Harvest Ville: trophies (owner, 30 Oct 2026)

Kanor makes these for the house. The game shows a simple stand-in until these arrive: save them in art-src/trophies/ with these names and run
`node tools/art/trophies-import.js`.

## 1. `trophy_antler.png` (1024x1024)
```
Cozy farming game art in a smooth, hand-painted cartoon style like FarmVille and Hay Day: rounded friendly shapes, soft shading with gentle highlights, clean dark-brown outlines, bright warm colors, soft light from the top-left. No text, no letters, no watermark, no border, no drop shadow box. Seen straight on, as it hangs on a wall in a top-down farm game. One single wall trophy, centered, filling most of the picture: a pair of small brown deer antlers mounted on a round polished wooden plaque. Friendly and simple, no blood. Isolated on a fully transparent background (PNG with alpha). If transparency is not possible, use a flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background with nothing else on it.
```
