# Harvest Ville: animals redrawn from the game's angle (owner, 30 Oct 2026)

Why: the old animals are flat side views, so they look pasted on the top-down world. These are drawn from above at the same angle as the farmer,
with walking poses toward you, away from you and to the side.

Every picture: **1024x1536 (tall)**. Save it with the file name shown. Do the wild animals first; the rest can come later.

## Wild animals (Pulag and Makiling)

### 1. `wild_rabbit.png`
```
Cozy farming game art in a smooth, hand-painted cartoon style like FarmVille and Hay Day: rounded friendly shapes, soft shading with gentle highlights, clean dark-brown outlines, bright warm colors, soft light from the top-left. No text, no letters, no watermark, no border, no drop shadow box. Drawn for a top-down three-quarter view game: the camera looks DOWN at the animal from above at about 45 degrees, so you always see the top of its back and head as well as its body, exactly like the farmer character in the same game. NOT a flat side view, NOT a flat front view. A sprite sheet arranged as a neat grid of 3 columns by 4 rows of equal cells, one pose per cell, each centered in its cell with clear empty space around it. The same animal in every cell: identical design, colors, size and proportions, standing on the same invisible ground line near the bottom of each cell, nothing touching the cell edges. No lines between cells. The animal: a small wild brown rabbit with a white fluffy tail and long ears. Row 1: walking toward the camera (facing down, front-and-top view). Row 2: walking away from the camera (facing up, back-and-top view). Row 3: walking to the LEFT (three-quarter side view from above). In rows 1 to 3 the columns are: standing still, mid-step with one leg forward, mid-step with the other leg forward. Row 4, left to right: sitting up alert with ears raised, facing the camera; eating grass with its head down, facing left; leaping away fast to the left, stretched out mid-jump. No shadow on the ground (the game adds its own). Isolated on a fully transparent background (PNG with alpha). If transparency is not possible, use a flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background with nothing else on it.
```
