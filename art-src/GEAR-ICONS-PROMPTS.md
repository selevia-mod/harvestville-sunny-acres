# Harvest Ville: Hunter's gear icons (owner, 5 Nov 2026)

Kanor's Buy tab shows the Camo Cloak, the Quiet Boots and the Big Quiver with simple stand-in icons drawn in code
(tools/art/gear-icons.js). The Fire Charm next to them is painted. This is one picture with all three, painted to match.

Make it with the prompt below and save it as **`art-src/hunt2/icons_gear.png`** (1536x1024, wide). Attach
`art-src/icons/gear_charm.png` (the Fire Charm) as the style to match. If the background comes out magenta or white
instead of see-through, that's okay too. Then: `node tools/art/gear-import.js`.
