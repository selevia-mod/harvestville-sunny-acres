# Farm Raids: the new pictures (owner, 9 Nov 2026; docs/raids.md)

Six raiders already have pictures (goat, cat, carabao, monkey, boar, rooster, bayawak are in the game). New: the crows,
the guard goose, the Raid Camp, six defenses and the raid icons. Transparent background (or flat magenta #FF00FF).
Save under `art-src/raids/` with the file names shown, and send them.
