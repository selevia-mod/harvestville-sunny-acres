# Raider animation sheets (owner, 10 Nov 2026: "even more smooth animation" and "there should also front and back")

24 pictures: 3 per raider (side, front, back). Each is a 4 x 4 animation sheet, 1536 x 1536, every frame in the SAME direction:
- `-side`: facing LEFT (the game mirrors it for right)
- `-front`: coming toward you (moving down the screen)
- `-back`: going away (moving up the screen)

The 4 rows are the same in all three:
- Row 1: run (the crow: fly) cycle, 4 frames
- Row 2: attack, 4 frames
- Row 3: run (fly) carrying loot, 4 frames
- Row 4: hurt, knocked back, knocked out, idle

For each: paste its prompt and ATTACH `references/<raider>-reference.png`. For the front and back sheets, ALSO attach the finished side sheet once you have it, so all three match.
Save as `<raider>-side.png`, `<raider>-front.png`, `<raider>-back.png` (goat, crow, cat, carabao, monkey, boar, rooster, bayawak) in `art-src/raider-anim/`. Send them in batches (zips are fine). Make the side one first for each raider.
No dust, sparks or stars in the pictures: the game draws its own on top.

### 1. raider-anim/goat-side.png  (1536x1536)  attach references/goat-reference.png

```
Cozy farming game art in a smooth, hand-painted cartoon style like FarmVille and Hay Day: rounded friendly shapes, soft shading with gentle highlights, clean dark-brown outlines, bright warm colors, soft light from the top-left. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border, no drop shadow box. Drawn for a top-down three-quarter view game: the camera looks DOWN at the animal from above at about 45 degrees. An ANIMATION sprite sheet: a neat grid of 4 columns by 4 rows of equal cells, one frame per cell, each centered in its cell with clear empty space around it, nothing touching the cell edges. No lines between cells. EXACTLY the same animal in every cell as in the attached reference picture (which shows it from the side): identical design, colors, markings, size and proportions, same scale in every frame. EVERY frame is the animal seen from the SIDE, facing and moving to the LEFT (three-quarter side view from above). Every frame stands on the same invisible ground line near the bottom of its cell, so the frames line up when played in a row. Each row is ONE smooth animation: the frames follow each other closely, only the body, legs (or wings) change between them. NO effects drawn: no dust clouds, no sparks, no impact stars, no motion lines, no speed lines, no swirl stars, no shadows on the ground (the game adds all of these). Cute and funny, never scary or gory. Isolated on a fully transparent background (PNG with alpha). Row 2, ATTACK, 4 frames: 1) wind-up: crouching back, weight back; 2) launch: springing forward; 3) the hit, charging with its head down and BUTTING with its horns, at full stretch; 4) recover: landing back and settling. Row 3, RUN WITH LOOT, 4 frames: the same run cycle as row 1, carrying a leafy turnip in its mouth, a happy sneaky grin. Row 4, left to right: 1) hurt: flinching back, eyes squeezed shut; 2) knocked off balance, tumbling backwards; 3) knocked out, lying on its side, eyes drawn as spirals (no stars); 4) standing idle and alert.
```
