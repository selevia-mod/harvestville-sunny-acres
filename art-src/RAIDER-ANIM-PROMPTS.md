# Raider animation sheets (owner, 10 Nov 2026: "create new art, even for all the raiders, for even more smooth animation")

8 pictures, one per raider. Each is a 4 x 4 animation sheet, 1536 x 1536, facing LEFT:
- Row 1: run (the crow: fly) cycle, 4 frames
- Row 2: attack, 4 frames
- Row 3: run (fly) carrying loot, 4 frames
- Row 4: hurt, knocked back, knocked out, idle

For each: paste its prompt and ATTACH its reference picture from `references/` (your battle art, so the look matches).
Save as `<raider>.png` (goat, crow, cat, carabao, monkey, boar, rooster, bayawak) in `art-src/raider-anim/` and send them (a zip is fine).
No dust, sparks or stars in the pictures: the game draws its own on top.

### 1. raider-anim/goat.png  (1536x1536)  attach references/goat-reference.png

```
Cozy farming game art in a smooth, hand-painted cartoon style like FarmVille and Hay Day: rounded friendly shapes, soft shading with gentle highlights, clean dark-brown outlines, bright warm colors, soft light from the top-left. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border, no drop shadow box. Drawn for a top-down three-quarter view game: the camera looks DOWN at the animal from above at about 45 degrees. An ANIMATION sprite sheet: a neat grid of 4 columns by 4 rows of equal cells, one frame per cell, each centered in its cell with clear empty space around it, nothing touching the cell edges. No lines between cells. EXACTLY the same animal in every cell as in the attached reference picture: identical design, colors, markings, size and proportions, same scale in every frame. Every frame faces LEFT. Every ground frame stands on the same invisible ground line near the bottom of its cell, so the frames line up when played in a row. Each row is ONE smooth animation: the frames follow each other closely, only the body and legs change between them. NO effects drawn: no dust clouds, no sparks, no impact stars, no motion lines, no speed lines, no swirl stars, no shadows on the ground (the game adds all of these). Cute and funny, never scary or gory. Isolated on a fully transparent background (PNG with alpha). If transparency is not possible, use a flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background with nothing else on it. The animal: a white farm goat with small horns. Row 1, RUN cycle, 4 frames: 1) contact: front legs reaching far forward, back legs stretched behind; 2) passing: legs gathered under the body, body at its lowest; 3) push-off: back legs pushing off, body rising; 4) flight: all four feet off the ground, body at its highest. Row 2, ATTACK, 4 frames: 1) wind-up: crouching back on its hind legs, weight back, eyes on the target ahead; 2) launch: springing forward; 3) the hit, charging with its head down and BUTTING with its horns, at full stretch; 4) recover: landing back and settling, ready to go again. Row 3, RUN WITH LOOT, 4 frames: the same run cycle as row 1, carrying a leafy turnip, a happy sneaky grin. Row 4, left to right: 1) hurt: flinching back, eyes squeezed shut; 2) knocked off balance, tumbling backwards; 3) knocked out, lying on its side, eyes drawn as spirals (no stars); 4) standing idle and alert, looking left.
```
