Tips:
- Furniture grids: the pieces must sit in a clear grid (4 across) with empty space between them, on a transparent or plain magenta background, in the order listed.
- Wallpapers and floors: 5 across, 2 rows, last cell empty.
- Houses: front-facing, door in the middle.
