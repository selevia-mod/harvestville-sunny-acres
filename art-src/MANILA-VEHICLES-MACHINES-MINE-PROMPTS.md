```

Seen from a top-down three-quarter view (camera looking down at about 45 degrees). No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border, no drop shadow box. One single game prop. Isolated on a fully transparent background (PNG with alpha). If transparency is not possible, use a flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background with nothing else on it. Keep the whole object inside the picture with some empty margin, its base along the bottom of the picture.
```
