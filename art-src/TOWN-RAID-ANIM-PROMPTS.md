# Town Raid attackers: animation sheets (owner, 10 Nov 2026: "How about the town raid attacker, lets generate arts for them too")

The Town Raid has 4 attackers: Giant Boars, Crows, Lava Sparks and Typhoon debris.
- **Crows:** no new art needed. They use the raider crow sheets you just made (side, front, back).
- **Giant Boar:** 3 sheets (side, front, back), it walks on the ground.
- **Lava Spark:** 1 sheet (it floats and is round, so one direction is enough).
- **Typhoon debris:** 1 sheet (spinning things look the same from every side).

5 pictures in all, 1536 x 1536 each. Paste the prompt and attach the reference picture named in its title. For the Giant Boar's front and back, also attach its finished side sheet.
Save as named in `art-src/town-raid-anim/` and send them.
