### Prompt (paste after attaching both)
```
Cozy farming game art in a smooth, hand-painted cartoon style like FarmVille and Hay Day: rounded friendly shapes, soft shading with gentle highlights, clean dark-brown outlines, bright warm colors, soft light from the top-left. The camera is only a LITTLE above, looking down at about 30 degrees: exactly the same camera height as the farmer in Image 1 (use Image 1 ONLY for the camera height; do not draw the farmer). Image 2 shows the town plaza this goes in (use it ONLY for the style and colors; do not copy anything from it): it must match the red-and-white striped market stalls, the stone fountain and the red-tiled cottage there.

Draw ONE building: an open-air town MARKET HALL, seen straight from the front, perfectly symmetrical left to right, wide and low (about one and a half times as wide as it is tall). A big warm red clay-tile roof with a gentle peak in the middle and wide eaves, held up by four thick honey-brown wooden posts (two at the front corners, two at the back) on small grey stone bases, with carved wooden brackets under the eaves. Along the front edge of the roof hangs a short scalloped red-and-white striped valance. Under the eaves: a few woven baskets of vegetables and fruit, a string of small warm lanterns and two little potted flower boxes hanging from the beams. In the middle of the front of the roof, a wide BLANK cream wooden sign board with a dark wood frame: the board is completely empty, no letters on it.

UNDER the roof the hall is EMPTY AND OPEN: no stalls, no tables, no people, no floor, no ground, no grass, no stones; between the posts is fully transparent, so the game's own stalls show through underneath. The roof fills the top half of the picture; the posts go down to the bottom edge of the picture.

No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no border, no drop shadow box, no ground shadow. Nothing is bent, curved or fish-eyed: straight lines stay straight. Isolated on a fully transparent background (PNG with alpha). If transparency is not possible, use a flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background with nothing else on it, also between the posts. Keep the whole building inside the picture with a little empty margin at the left, right and top.
```
